## How to use this list

1. **Daily**: solve the day's 15 problems.
2. **Review**: re-solve the day's hards 1 week later.
3. **Spaced repetition**: re-solve all easy/medium after 30 days.
4. **Mock interview**: pick 1 problem per day, time yourself at 30 min.

> Track which problems you can solve without hints. Aim for ≥ 80% on the first try.
